Overview

This game was created as a first-semester university assignment and combines puzzle traversal with a lightweight story cutscene style.

My Role

I implemented the platform behavior logic, puzzle interactions, and progression flow based on color-driven movement rules.

Key Features

  • Top-down logic puzzles built around moving platforms.
  • Color-coded platform behavior with distinct movement patterns.
  • Puzzle combinations requiring route planning and adaptive timing.
