Overview

This project focuses on a classic tower defense loop with endless wave progression and increasing encounter complexity.

My Role

I worked on gameplay systems supporting wave scaling, tower behavior variation, and enemy stat diversity for strategic decision making.

Key Features

  • Progressively increasing wave difficulty through scalable enemy values.
  • Multiple tower types with distinct mechanics and tactical trade-offs.
  • Enemy variants differentiated by health, armor, and movement speed.
