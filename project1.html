Overview

Glass Thoughts is my most extensive project to date. It combines visual novel storytelling with interactive gameplay, and it became the practical foundation for my engineering thesis work.

My Role

I worked as programmer and game designer, while coordinating development with artists, a writer, and a sound designer. I implemented gameplay features, built the narrative tooling layer, and maintained the production workflow in Unity.

Key Features

  • Custom command-based dialogue and narrative scripting system inside Unity.
  • Branching story flow with scene transitions and player choices.
  • Integrated minigames connected to story progression.
  • Production-oriented architecture designed for iterative content work.
