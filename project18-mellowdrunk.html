Overview

Mellowdrunk is a 3D platformer made during HelloGameJam 2025, a 24-hour on-site game jam in Wroclaw. The player controls a bear moving through a compact platforming level with limited direct input. The core gameplay is built around power-ups that modify the character's available move set, animation state, and behavior.

My Role

I worked as the main gameplay programmer. My responsibilities focused on implementing the core gameplay loop, player behavior, and power-up logic using Unreal Engine Blueprints within the 24-hour time limit.

This was one of the more structured game jam teams I have worked with. The team included people with professional industry experience, and a second programmer focused on code integration, merge management, and QA support rather than feature development. This kept the workflow stable throughout the jam and contributed directly to the quality of the final build.

Key Features

  • 3D platformer gameplay built around limited player input.
  • Power-ups that change character behavior, animation state, and available actions.
  • Core gameplay loop prototyped and implemented within a 24-hour production window.
  • Team workflow supported by dedicated code integration, technical review, and QA.
