Overview

This project builds on the narrative interpreter architecture from Glass Thoughts and expands it for a new production with richer presentation features.

My Role

I am extending the custom command-based dialogue and narrative scripting system, integrating Live2D support, and implementing scene control and progression workflows in Unity.

Key Features

  • Custom command-based narrative scripting stack with branching story flow and player choices.
  • Live2D Cubism integration for animated character presentation.
  • Scene control and event-driven progression connected to narrative state.
  • Cooking minigame module tied to story pacing.
