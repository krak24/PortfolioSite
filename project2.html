Overview

This is an in-development Unreal Engine prototype built around structured study of multiplayer shooter architecture in C++. The project follows a course-based learning path while also serving as a testbed for AI systems and gameplay ideas I want to develop further.

The goal is not only to reproduce course mechanics, but to understand how gameplay systems interact in a networked environment and how AI logic can be adapted for multiplayer conditions.

My Role

I am using this project to deepen my understanding of Unreal Engine C++ multiplayer programming. Alongside the course implementation, I am testing AI behavior that builds on concepts I already know, including decision-making, navigation, and state management, and adapting them to work with networked players.

I am also exploring two directions for future development: an AI enemy system influenced by player behavior and environmental context, loosely inspired by director-driven enemy design, and a procedural world generation system built around my own gameplay rules and level design assumptions using Unreal Engine tools.

Key Features

  • Unreal Engine C++ multiplayer shooter prototype based on structured course study.
  • Networked gameplay systems implemented and tested as part of an active learning process.
  • AI behavior experiments adapted for multiplayer conditions.
  • Planned enemy AI that responds to player input and environmental context.
  • Planned procedural world generation based on gameplay-focused design rules.
