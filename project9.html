Game Jam

Overview

Built during Bit Jam Wroclaw, this project challenges players to climb upward with a bounce-like movement system while an advancing hazard constantly pushes progression.

My Role

I contributed to gameplay implementation and iteration under strict jam time constraints, focusing on movement feel and moment-to-moment pressure.

Key Features

  • Vertical platforming inspired by Jump King and Icy Tower style challenge curves.
  • Tail-bounce movement requiring timing and precision.
  • Rising fire mechanic with collectible water drops that slow hazard progression.
